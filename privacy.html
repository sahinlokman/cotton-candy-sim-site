Privacy policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

About this policy

Cotton Candy Sim is a general-audience creative game for iPhone and iPad provided by Lokman Şahin. This policy covers the app and this website. Contact lokman782782@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.

Game data on your device

Your unfinished candy and preferences are stored on your device. The app does not require a separate game account or ask for your name, email address, date of birth, precise location, photos, or microphone access. This version does not provide iCloud game synchronization or a developer-hosted gallery. Removing the app removes local game data, subject to Apple’s backup behavior.

Advertising and your choices

Free use may show a Google AdMob banner on the home screen. Banners are hidden during gameplay and while an active ad-free subscription is recognized. The app requests non-personalized ads, disables the Google publisher first-party identifier, and does not request Apple’s cross-app tracking permission. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing: Google’s SDK may handle IP addresses (including approximate location), device or app identifiers, ad interactions, crash reports, and performance information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and maintain its service.

Google’s User Messaging Platform handles regional consent messages and determines whether an ad request is permitted. Where available, you can revisit your choices in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Refusing consent does not prevent you from playing. An ad-free subscription removes advertising while active; it does not remove the purchase validation needed to maintain that subscription. See Google’s privacy policy and Google’s advertising information.

Monthly subscriptions and RevenueCat

Apple processes optional monthly ad-free subscriptions and payments. RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription records and an automatically generated anonymous app-user identifier to validate purchases, restore access, keep subscription status up to date, and provide subscription reporting. We do not associate this identifier with a name or an app account. We do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password. Apple and RevenueCat retain the records needed to operate these services under their policies. See Apple’s privacy policy and RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

On-device check

Purchase access and external links include an on-device arithmetic check to reduce accidental actions. Its answers are not stored or transmitted. This is not identity or age verification and is not a substitute for legally required parental consent.

Support, retention, and your rights

If you email us, we receive the address and message you provide and use them to handle your request. Please do not send passwords, payment information, or unnecessary personal details. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. You can contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information we hold, or to exercise rights available in your region. Local game data can be removed from your device. Subscription and advertising providers may retain transaction, security, or service records under their own policies; deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Children

The app is for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through support, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where applicable.

This website and international processing

This static website uses no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, forms, or tracking cookies added by us. GitHub Pages may process technical information such as IP addresses for hosting and security under GitHub’s privacy statement. Our service providers may process information in other countries under their policies and applicable safeguards. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. We update this page when practices change and display the revision date above.

Website analytics

This website uses self-hosted Umami at stats.roomivo.online to measure page visits. The tracker sends page metadata, browser language and screen size; URL query strings and fragments are excluded. It does not read form fields, identify users, set analytics cookies or record sessions. Analytics can be blocked by your browser and respects Do Not Track when available. This website measurement is separate from any data processing described above for the app.
