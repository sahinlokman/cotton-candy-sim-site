Privacy policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Who we are

Cotton Candy Sim is provided by Lokman Şahin. This policy covers the children’s edition of the iPhone and iPad app and this website. Parents and guardians can contact lokman782782@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.

A game without advertising or tracking

The children’s edition contains no advertising, third-party analytics, RevenueCat, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking. We do not ask children for a name, email address, date of birth, location, photos, microphone access, or a separate game account. We do not operate a server that receives gameplay data.

Progress on your device and in iCloud

Game progress, creations, preferences, and achievements are stored on the device. When iCloud is available, progress can synchronize using Apple’s iCloud services associated with your Apple Account. This does not give us access to your Apple Account credentials. You can manage iCloud through your device settings. Removing the app deletes its local data subject to Apple’s backup behavior; iCloud copies and backups may remain until removed through Apple’s controls.

Optional purchases through Apple

A parent may purchase an annual Premium subscription using Apple In-App Purchase. The app uses StoreKit to retrieve prices and verify signed purchase transactions and active subscription access, including restores. These checks happen through Apple; we do not receive payment-card details or send purchase records to a third-party subscription service. Apple retains transaction records under its own policies. Subscription management and cancellation are available in Apple Account settings.

Parent checks and sharing

Purchases, restore actions, external websites, and image sharing require a fresh parent check. The check is processed on the device and is not stored or transmitted. It is a safeguard against accidental actions, not identity verification or a substitute for legally required parental consent. A parent can choose to share a locally generated candy image using the system share sheet. Images are not uploaded automatically. The selected destination applies its own privacy practices.

Support and privacy requests

If a parent emails us, we receive the address and message voluntarily provided and use them to answer the request. Please do not include children’s personal details, passwords, or payment information. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. You may request access, correction, or deletion of correspondence by contacting us. Game progress is held on your device and, if enabled, in your iCloud account; we cannot retrieve it from a developer server.

Children’s privacy

This edition is designed for children and their families. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the game or sell or share it for targeted advertising. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, a parent or guardian should contact us so we can take appropriate steps, including deletion where applicable.

This website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, forms, or tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical request information such as IP addresses for hosting and security under GitHub’s privacy statement. Opening an email link uses your email provider. Apple services are covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Security, processing, and changes

Information handled by Apple, GitHub, and email providers may be processed in other countries under their policies. We use the platform protections available to us, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. We update this page when practices change and show the revised date below. Parents may contact us about rights available in their region.
