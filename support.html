Frequently asked questions

How can I protect my progress?

Sign into iCloud with the same Apple Account and check Cloud sync in game Settings. If iCloud is unavailable, progress stays on your device. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting.

How do I restore Premium?

Open Settings → Restore purchases and complete the parent check. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Use Subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Deleting the game does not cancel a subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

Will my child see ads?

No. Both free and Premium play in the children’s edition have no ads.

What does Premium include?

While the annual subscription is active, Premium provides double daily rewards, the Starlight Lounge, and its decorations. The price is shown in your App Store currency before purchase.
