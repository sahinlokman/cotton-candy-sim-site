Contact lokman782782@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

What is included?

All 4 shapes, 12 flavors, and 40 decorations and accessories are available without a subscription. Choose, pour, wrap, decorate, then tap or swipe to pretend to eat your candy. Assisted controls, reduced motion, music, sound, and haptics can be adjusted in Settings.

Ads and the monthly subscription

Free use may show a home-screen banner. The optional monthly subscription removes ads while active. Its US base price is USD 4.99; your local App Store price appears before purchase. Open Settings → Remove ads, complete the on-device check, and choose Subscribe monthly.

Restore or cancel

To restore, open the same subscription screen and select Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. To cancel or manage renewal, use Manage subscription or your device’s Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel billing. Apple handles refund requests at Report a Problem.

Where is my progress?

Your unfinished candy and preferences are saved locally on this device. This version has no iCloud progress synchronization. Removing the app may remove this progress.

Privacy choices

Where available, Settings → Ad privacy choices reopens the regional consent form. See the privacy policy for Google AdMob and RevenueCat data processing details.
